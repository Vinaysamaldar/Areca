Project Objective

To design and deploy an automated, end-to-end deep learning web application capable of classifying arecanut palm diseases from photographic imagery in real time, delivering localized organic and chemical remedy recommendations directly to farmers and extension workers.

Agronomic Motivation

Arecanut (Areca catechu) is a premier commercial cash crop in Karnataka (Malnad and coastal belts). Fungal outbreaks like Koleroga cause catastrophic fruit rot within days of heavy monsoon downpours, leading to massive financial losses. Due to scarcity of skilled tree-climbers, automated non-invasive visual screening provides essential early intervention.
