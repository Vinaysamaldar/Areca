            Photo Quality Guidelines

            Follow these tips for >95% model classification accuracy

            Optimal Sunlight Photograph during daylight hours. Avoid intense camera flash reflections or deep dark canopy shadows.
            Frame & Proximity Hold camera 15-30 cm away from the infected leaf, fruit bunch, or stem area. Fill 70% of the frame with the plant tissue.
            Strictly Plant Only Do not include faces, humans, livestock, or ground clutter. The AI strictly validates Arecanut palm imagery.
