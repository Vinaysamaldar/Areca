Annual Plantation Spray Calendar

Timely prophylactic operations to preempt monsoonal rot and stress

May - June Critical

Pre-Monsoon Preparation

Preventive barrier against Koleroga (Mahali) rot spores before heavy showers begin.

  • 1% Bordeaux Mixture Spray: Spray all bunches and crowns thoroughly. Add rosin soap/sticker.
  • Drainage Cleansing: Clear plantation drainage channels to prevent root-zone water stagnation.
Target: Nut Koleroga & Bud Rot
July - August Monsoon

Southwest Monsoon Peak

Maintain fungicide shield during continuous cloudy days and high relative humidity.

  • Second Spray: 40-45 days after 1st spray during a break in rain.
  • Fallen Nut Destruction: Collect and bury dropped rotten nuts in deep trenches to halt spore drift.
Target: Koleroga & Secondary Rot
Sept - November Recovery

Post-Monsoon & Trunk Care

Root zone nutrition replenishment, basal stem check, and Yellow Leaf foliar drenching.

  • Trunk Lesion Dressing: Chisel infected bark for Stem Bleeding; apply Bordeaux paste or hot coal tar.
  • Bio-Agent Drenching: Basin application of Trichoderma enriched neem cake + FYM.
Target: Stem Bleeding & Anabe Foot Rot
Dec - April Summer

Summer Moisture & Root Health

Prevent sun scorching of the trunk and eliminate root grubs during dry weather.

  • Trunk White-Washing: Paint southwest exposed trunks with lime to reflect intense solar radiation.
  • Basin Mulching: Spread green or dry mulch across the 1-meter palm radius to conserve soil moisture.
Target: Sun Scorch, Nut Split & Root Grub
